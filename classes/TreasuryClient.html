    Class TreasuryClient

    Client for one treasury contract.

    • Reads (get*) simulate a call and return decoded data. They need no account or signature.
    • Writes (propose*, approve*, …) return a PreparedTransaction: simulated, fee-estimated and ready to sign. Nothing is sent until you call sign/submit/signAndSubmit.

    Use connect for a signer-bound variant whose writes sign and submit immediately.
